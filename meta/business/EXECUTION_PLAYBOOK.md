## 🚧 COMMON OBSTACLES & SOLUTIONS

### Obstacle: "Nobody responds to my emails"

**Why**: Your email is too long or too sales-y

**Fix**: Make it 3 sentences:
```
"Saw your work on X. Built a tool that does Y. 
Would you try it on Z problem from your paper?"
```

---

### Obstacle: "People try it but don't pay"

**Why**: Not enough value OR wrong pricing OR wrong positioning

**Fix**: 
1. Ask users: "What would make this worth $49/month to you?"
2. Build that feature
3. Re-approach them

---

### Obstacle: "I don't know what to build next"

**Why**: You're building what YOU think is cool, not what users need

**Fix**:
1. Talk to your 10 users
2. Ask: "What's the biggest pain point?"
3. Build only that
4. Repeat

---

### Obstacle: "I'm not good at sales/marketing"

**Why**: You think sales = being pushy

**Reality**: Sales = helping people solve problems

**Fix**: 
- Don't "sell"
- Just help people
- "Here's a tool that might help with your problem"
- If it helps, they'll pay

---

### Obstacle: "I'm afraid to charge money"

**Why**: Imposter syndrome + don't want to seem greedy

**Reality**: If you don't charge, people don't value it

**Fix**: 
- Start small: $29/month
- Frame it as "support development"
- Offer money-back guarantee
- You'll find people are happy to pay for value
