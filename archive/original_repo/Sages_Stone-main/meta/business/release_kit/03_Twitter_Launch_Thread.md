## Response Templates for Common Reactions

**"This is just genetic algorithms"**
Key difference: GAs optimize toward a fitness function you define. RCF has no fitness function—only survival time under adversarial pressure. Selection without teleology.

**"Can this discover real physics?"**
Not yet. It distinguishes robust from brittle constraints, but doesn't yet bootstrap from minimal rules to conservation laws. That's the research goal.

**"Sounds like pseudoscience"**
Fair concern. Two clarifications: (1) This is computational philosophy, not physics claims. (2) The hypothesis is methodological ("survival pressure reveals fundamentality") not metaphysical ("reality IS survival").

**"Why not just use physics simulators?"**
Physics simulators assume the laws you're trying to discover. RCF inverts: start with minimal constraints, test survival, extract laws. It's constraint archaeology.

**"LawScore seems arbitrary"**
It's v1. Design choice: reward longevity and cross-dimensional stability, penalize instability. Open to better formulations. What would you suggest?

**"This is really cool!"**
Thanks! Try it yourself—the React interface makes it easy to experiment. Would love to hear what you discover.
