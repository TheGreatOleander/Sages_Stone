## Decision Points

### Month 3: Assess Traction
**If good traction (5+ customers, $25k+ revenue):**
→ Go full-time, hire help, raise prices

**If moderate traction (2-3 customers, $10k revenue):**
→ Continue part-time, focus on case studies

**If poor traction (0-1 customers, <$5k revenue):**
→ Pivot strategy, try different market, or shelve for now

### Month 6: Growth Decision
**If growing (10+ customers, $50k+ revenue):**
→ Build team, consider SaaS platform, raise seed funding

**If plateaued (5 customers, $20k revenue):**
→ Maintain as lifestyle business, or pivot to services

**If declining:**
→ Exit gracefully, open source everything, move on
