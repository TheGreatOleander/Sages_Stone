## Demos

Demos are first-class value artifacts.

Each demo is runnable and is designed to showcase a single core idea or behavior of the system.

Demos are organized by intent:
- **Canonical demos** define expected and stable system behavior. These act as executable specifications and are the recommended starting point.
- **Exploratory demos** examine dynamics, constraints, observers, and edge cases. These demos are intentionally experimental and may evolve over time.

Together, the demos form a runnable map of the system’s behavior, from foundational guarantees to emergent and stress-tested phenomena.
