## POST-PRODUCTION NOTES

### Music:
- Ambient, philosophical tone (like Arrival soundtrack)
- Build tension during collapse sequences
- Calm, contemplative during philosophy sections
- No music during Watts quote (silence is powerful)

### Color Grading:
- Cool blues/purples for phase space visualizations
- Warm oranges/reds for collapse moments
- Stark black/white for philosophy sections

### Text Overlays:
- Minimal, sans-serif font
- Key quotes appear briefly, don't linger
- Avoid clutter—let visuals speak

### Pacing:
- Slow down during philosophy (let ideas breathe)
- Speed up during technical demos (keep energy)
- Pause after key insights (let viewer process)

### B-Roll Ideas:
- Time-lapses of constraint tournaments (sped up 4x)
- Animated mathematical equations dissolving into particles
- Abstract 3D renders of apple meshes rotating

### Thumbnail:
Option A: Collapse cartography (colorful, eye-catching)
Option B: "Reality is what survives pressure" text over dark background
Option C: Split-screen: elegant equation vs. messy simulation

- [ ] Close unnecessary apps

- [ ] Have script on secondary monitor

- [ ] Record room tone (60 seconds silence for noise reduction)

### After Recording:
