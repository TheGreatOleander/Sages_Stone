## Executive Summary

**Reality Constraint Fuzzer (RCF)** is positioned as a specialized constraint-preservation layer for autonomous and safety-critical systems. The dual-license model (AGPL + Commercial) enables:

- **Open Research Community**: Free academic/research use builds credibility and surface area
- **Commercial Revenue**: Companies integrating RCF into proprietary products pay for freedom from AGPL obligations
- **Consulting/Services**: Domain-specific implementations for aerospace, robotics, industrial control

**Target 2025 Revenue**: $50k-150k (bootstrapped)  
**Target 2026 Revenue**: $250k-500k (with initial enterprise customers)

## Market Positioning

### What RCF Is
A **constraint-preserving stabilization primitive** - not a simulator, not an optimizer, but a law-enforcement layer that prevents catastrophic drift in autonomous systems.

### Competitive Advantage
- Model-agnostic (works with any planner/controller)
- Deterministic and auditable
- Lightweight (embeddable in resource-constrained environments)
- Language-agnostic (Python, C, Rust, etc.)

1. **Aerospace & Defense** ($$$)

   - **Willingness to pay**: Medium-high

## Sales Process

### Typical Customer Journey

1. **Discover** (AGPL version)
   - GitHub, blog posts, papers, HN/Reddit

2. **Evaluate** (free trial)
   - Download reference implementation
   - Run simulations on their data
   - Identify constraint violations

3. **Pilot** (paid or subsidized)
   - Integrate into one system
   - Measure reduction in failures/oscillations
   - Internal champion emerges

4. **Purchase** (commercial license)
   - Legal review of AGPL vs. commercial terms
   - Negotiation on pricing/scope
   - Contract signed

5. **Expand** (upsell)
   - Additional applications
   - Enterprise license upgrade
   - Ongoing consulting

**Average Sales Cycle**: 3-9 months (enterprise), 1-3 months (startup)

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk: "Market is too niche"
**Mitigation**: 
- Start with highest-value segments (aerospace/defense)
- Expand to adjacent markets once proven
- Build open-source community for long-term viability

### Risk: "AGPL is too scary for companies"
**Mitigation**:
- Clear dual-license messaging
- Easy commercial license terms
- Transparent pricing

### Risk: "Competitors emerge"
**Mitigation**:
- Strong open-source community (network effects)
- Domain expertise and thought leadership
- First-mover advantage in specific verticals

### Risk: "Can't explain value prop"
**Mitigation**:
- Case studies showing prevented failures
- Benchmark against existing approaches
- Demo videos and interactive examples
