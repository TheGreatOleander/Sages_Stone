## Email Best Practices

✅ **Do:**
- Personalize every email (reference specific projects/papers)
- Lead with their problem, not your solution
- Include one concrete metric/result
- Keep under 150 words
- Make the ask crystal clear

❌ **Don't:**
- Generic mass emails
- Attach files in first contact
- Oversell or make unrealistic claims
- Use marketing jargon
- Send more than 2 follow-ups
